I am a PhD student in **Economic History** at the **London School of Economics and Political Science (LSE)**. I began my PhD in September 2024 and am supervised by [Prof. Patrick Wallis](https://www.lse.ac.uk/people/patrick-wallis) and [Dr Melanie Meng Xue](https://www.melaniexue.net/).

My research interests lie at the intersection of economic history, welfare, and public economics. I am particularly interested in how public decision-making, individual motivations and behaviour (including altruism), and institutions shape long-run economic and social change.

My PhD dissertation examines the evolution of poor relief and private charity in England, focusing on 1600–1786. It asks why England developed a locally administered, publicly financed system of poor relief that expanded over more than 200 years; how the allocation of assistance became increasingly selective in recipients and purposes as provision grew; and how public relief and private charity interacted over time.
