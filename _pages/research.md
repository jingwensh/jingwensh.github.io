My research draws on annual poor-relief expenditure data from **312 parishes across 42 English counties**, combining newly collected archival records with the parish series assembled by [Brodie Waddell (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1093/pastj/gtaa027). I have also digitised **167,465 individual disbursement entries** from parish accounts.

## Why Did Poor Relief Expand? Re-Examining the Growth of Parish Relief in England, 1600–1786

*Work in progress*

How did England develop such an extensive system of publicly financed poor relief, and what drove its long-run growth? The paper asks how temporary subsistence pressures, alongside local fiscal responses, contributed to the evolution of relief provision.

## Selective Welfare in Early Modern England: Poor Relief, Charity, and Regional Divergence, 1600–1786

*Work in progress*

How did assistance become more selective as welfare provision expanded? This paper traces changes in **who received support** and **the purposes for which it was provided**, comparing the evolution of public poor relief and private charity across regions.

## Did Poor Relief Supplement Wages before Speenhamland?

*Work in progress*

Did parish poor relief function partly as wage supplementation before Speenhamland? This paper asks whether relief responded differently to food-price shocks when wages were weak, distinguishing potential wage support from more general responses to subsistence pressure.
